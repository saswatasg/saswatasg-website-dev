At LiveKeeping, I worked on an AI image-generation integration for the Pro+ Send Greetings feature and a geo-segmented calendar of 27 occasions across five regional groupings. The supplied record reports +168% feature engagement; it does not establish the same increase in overall product retention or revenue.

## Give the feature a relevant moment

The feature helps a merchant create greetings for customers. Its product challenge is broader than generating an attractive image: the occasion, language and business context need to make the greeting useful at the moment someone wants to send it.

The documented work combines image generation with a regional festival calendar. This gives the product a structured source of relevant occasions rather than treating every user as if they follow the same national calendar. Five groupings provide a practical segmentation basis; they are not a claim to represent every local custom.

## Make the integration understandable

The supplied work describes the Nano Banana image model integration and its role in producing greeting artwork. The product flow brings together the selected occasion, business context and image generation. A user needs to understand what will be created and have a clear path to review the result.

An AI integration should be assessed through the task it helps complete. Generation availability alone does not show that someone created a usable card or sent it successfully. Those steps deserve separate instrumentation rather than one undifferentiated AI-use counter.

## Calendar design is part of the product

The 27-occasion calendar makes timing and relevance explicit. Regional mapping connects the schedule to the merchant’s context. The feature work also fits the broader lifecycle-messaging architecture, where priority, daily limits and conflicting messages need to be considered.

A useful notification is a prompt for a relevant task. Sending more reminders is not automatically better, and the calendar should not be interpreted as permission to contact every merchant for every occasion. The design needs a path from relevance to action, alongside controls for message fatigue.

## What the reported result means

The supplied record reports a 168% improvement in feature engagement. The public evidence does not provide the underlying event definition, denominator, cohort dates or attribution method. This case therefore keeps the label at feature engagement, rather than presenting it as an increase in DAU, revenue, retention or cards per user.

The earlier article included unsupported claims about eighteen months of usage, cards per active user, cache-hit rates and evergreen volume share. Those figures are not needed to explain the contribution and have been removed. A defensible result should survive without invented supporting precision.

## What I would measure next

The next evidence pack should define the engaged action and comparison period, then separate generation attempts, successful images, user edits and completed sends. Regional uptake and repeat use would help distinguish one-time curiosity from recurring usefulness. These are measurement questions, not results claimed here.

The delivered story is a feature integration and a structured occasion calendar, with a reported engagement outcome whose measurement scope remains limited in the public case.
